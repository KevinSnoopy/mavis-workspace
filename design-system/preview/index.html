The Compounding of Habits · 共 12 页 · 约 8 分钟

Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement. The same way that money multiplies through compound interest, the effects of your habits multiply as you repeat them.

习惯是自我提升的复利。金钱通过复利倍增，你的行为习惯也以同样的方式在重复中倍增。

People think they lack motivation. What they actually lack is clarity, and the truth is, they don't have a system in place.

人们以为缺少动力，其实缺少的是清晰的认识——事实上，他们没有一个成型的体系。

Getting one percent better every day counts for a lot when you look at it over the span of a year. But when you do the opposite, when you're one percent worse every day, it compounds.

You find yourself stuck in a rut so familiar that you can no longer see the rut you're in.
